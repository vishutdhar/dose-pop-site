Hard to miss. Easy to use.

  • Sounds even on silent

    In Settings, turn on Sound Even on Silent and allow it once. Reminders then play when the phone is muted or in a Focus mode, at a volume you choose.

  • A second alert if a dose is missed

    If a dose is not marked, a follow up alert comes after a wait you choose. The wait starts at 30 minutes.

  • Answer from the reminder

    Tap I Took It, Snooze or Skip right on the reminder. Snooze and Skip work without opening the app.

  • Big buttons, large print

    The buttons are large and easy to hit. The words grow with the iPhone text size setting, and the buttons stack when the text is at its largest.

  • A record of every dose

    History shows each dose and the time it was taken, so nobody has to guess whether a pill was already taken.

  • A warning before you run out

    Enter how many doses you have. When about a week is left, the Today screen says it is time to refill. For a medicine taken as needed, the warning comes at five doses.

  • Any schedule

    Every day, two or three times a day, every other day, chosen weekdays, as needed, or every few days, weeks or months.

  • Doses on the Home Screen

    Widgets show the day’s doses on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen, without opening the app.

Let one family member keep watch.

Caregiver Alerts stay off until the person taking the medicine turns them on and picks one family member or helper. Nothing is shared before that.

  • They see what was taken

    The names of the medicines, and whether each dose was taken that day.

  • Dosages are never shown

    They never see how much of each medicine is taken.

  • An email if a dose looks missed

    When a dose is still not marked after the wait, they get an email, so they can call and check in.

  • Stop sharing any time

    Sharing ends when you choose, and the link they were sent stops working.
